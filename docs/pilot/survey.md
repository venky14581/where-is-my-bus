# Pilot survey

Two short surveys (a Google Form is fine). The first, before the pilot,
also checks the problem statement, which has not been confirmed with day
scholars yet (the project spec). Anonymous; no names or roll numbers.

## Before the pilot (all day scholars on the pilot route)

1. How do you usually get to campus? (college bus / public bus / own vehicle / other)
2. On a usual morning, how long do you wait at your stop? (under 5 / 5–10 / 10–20 / over 20 minutes)
3. In the last month, how many times did you miss the college bus? (never / once / 2–3 / more)
4. When you missed it, what did you do? (public bus / auto from Ettimadai / someone dropped me / stayed home / other)
5. Did a missed bus make you miss a class? (never / once / more than once)
6. How do you hear about delays, breakdowns or changes today? (WhatsApp group / driver / office / I don't / other)
7. Hostellers and own-transport students: would you book an evening seat on a college bus to go home? (yes, per ride / yes, if free / no)
8. Which other towns or areas should college buses serve? (free text)

## After two weeks (pilot riders)

1. How many days a week did you open the app? (0 / 1–2 / 3–4 / 5–6)
2. How often was the arrival time right to within about 2 minutes? (almost always / usually / sometimes / rarely)
3. Did you wait less at your stop than before? (much less / a bit less / same / more)
4. Which notifications helped? (bus started / bus approaching / time to leave / delays / announcements / none)
5. Did you use missed-bus help or evening seats? If so, did it work? (free text)
6. Anything that did not work or was confusing? (free text)
7. Would you recommend it to another day scholar? (0–10)
8. Did you feel your privacy was respected? (yes / not sure / no; why?)
