# Privacy notice (pilot)

Draft for the transport office and the college to approve; translate to
Tamil before giving it out. It describes what the app actually does
(see `CLAUDE.md` and the module docs).

**Where Is My Bus** is being tried on one college bus route from
[start date] to [end date].

**What is shared:** only the **bus's** location, from the driver's phone,
while a trip is running. It goes to students and staff of the college who
sign in with a college account, and to anyone a student gives a "Share my
trip" link (that link stops working when the trip ends).

**Your location is never sent.** If you allow location, your phone uses it
only on your phone, to find the nearest stop when you miss your bus. For the
"time to leave" reminder you type in how many minutes you walk; that number
is stored, not where you live.

**What is stored about you:** your name, college email, route and stop,
language, notification choices, your bus pass status, when your pass or
ticket was scanned and at which stop, and any evening seat you book and pay
for. Payments in the pilot are in test mode: no real money is taken.

**What is stored about trips:** the time the bus reached each stop, not its
path. These are used to show arrival times, to learn usual delays, and for
the transport office's reports.

**Who can see it:** you see your own details. The transport office sees
passes, bookings, boardings and reports. Drivers see who boarded their bus
and who is waiting for it. Nobody else.

**SOS:** pressing SOS sends the bus's number and location to the transport
office.

**Questions or to have your data removed:** contact [transport office
contact]. After the pilot, data from students who do not continue is
deleted within [30] days.
